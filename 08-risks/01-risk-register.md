## The three risks that cannot be mitigated, only survived

| Risk | Why unmitigable | Survival strategy |
|---|---|---|
| **Armed conflict** | Outside the platform's control; can suspend an entire region | Geographic diversification; cash reserves; light assets; staff safety first |
| **A hostile policy change** | A ministry can prohibit the model overnight | Partnership; cooperative equity; legal structure flexibility |
| **Macro-financial collapse** | FX and inflation destroy unit economics | Local cost base; birr pricing; minimal foreign spend |

**Honest statement:** if any of these three occurs severely, the platform will likely fail. The plan
should be understood as *conditional on a stable-enough operating environment* — which is the same
condition every other business in the country faces.

## The register's blind spot, stated openly

Registers capture **known** risks. The failures that kill platforms are usually:

1. A risk nobody thought of (frequently: a person, not a system).
2. A risk that was known but assigned to no one.
3. A risk that was accepted because it was inconvenient to act on.

**Mitigation for the register itself:** every quarter, ask "what would have to be true for this to fail
in a way we have not listed?" and record the answer.
