## Rules for using this ledger

1. **Never cite a ⚪ figure as evidence.** It is an assumption to be tested.
2. **Never cite a 🟠 figure without the caveat.** It may be off by a factor of two.
3. **Check the date.** Most figures are from 2025–2026 and will age.
4. **Check for conflicts.** Where a source conflicts with another, both are recorded in
   [`../10-sources/03-figures-and-corrections.md`](../10-sources/03-figures-and-corrections.md).
5. **Update, don't overwrite.** When a figure changes, record the old value and the reason.

silently overwriting.

| 4 | Corruption rank **~99** | **38/100, rank 96/182** | TI CPI 2025 | 🟢 | Annual index update; score matters more than rank |
| 5 | Electricity access **~50%** | **65% any access; 44% Tier 1+; 29.3% grid** | World Bank/ESMAP 2023 | 🟢 | "Access" conflates grid, off-grid and unreliable supply |
| 6 | Account ownership **~35%** | **49% of adults** | Global Findex 2025 | 🟢 | Findex 2025 survey superseded earlier estimate |
| 7 | M-Pesa Ethiopia **"just launched"** | **5.2 m active users** (Oct 2026) | Safaricom | 🟡 | Launched Aug 2023; now at scale |
| 8 | GDP growth **6.5%** | **9.2%** (FY2025/26) | IMF ECF review | 🟡 | Post-rebasing and post-float revision |
| 9 | Inflation **~20%** | **15.3%** (Jul 2026) | NBE / IMF | 🟡 | Disinflation through 2026 |
