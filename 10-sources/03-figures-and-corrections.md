## 10. Figures deliberately left out

| Figure | Why excluded |
|---|---|
| Ethiopia e-commerce market size | Sources conflict by 400× (US$29.78 bn vs ~US$71 m) |
| "Number of farmers using digital platforms" | No reliable count; vendor claims overlap |
| Exact post-harvest loss rate | Range 15–30% too wide; country-level data weak |
| Precise ag credit demand | Single industry estimate; not verifiable |
| Smartphone penetration | Definition varies (connections vs. users vs. devices) |

**A repository that excludes unreliable figures is more useful than one that repeats them with false
precision.**
